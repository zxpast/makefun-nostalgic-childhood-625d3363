> The ice-cream truck played the same broken tune every July, and we ran barefoot with coins warm from our pockets, and the summer it stopped coming, nobody told us why.

The truck, the broken tune, the warm coins — and then, one summer, it simply never comes, and no adult ever explains why. Nothing is sentimentalised; the warm ache is the unexplained absence itself.
